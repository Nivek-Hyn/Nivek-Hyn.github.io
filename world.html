Behind the scene

Thanks for visiting!
This world is not built from scratch: it runs on the open-source engine and world of Bruno Simon's portfolio, with my own content in it.

Credits

Engine, world and 3D models: folio-2025 by Bruno Simon, under MIT license. Go see the original at bruno-simon.com.

Campus streets, buildings and mountains, hero skeletons and animations: KayKit City Builder Bits, Space Base Bits, Medieval Hexagon, Adventurers and Character Animations by Kay Lousberg, under CC0 license.

Music by Kounine (Linktree), under CC0 license. City interface and impact sounds: Interface Sounds and Sci-fi Sounds by Kenney, under CC0 license.

Stack

  • Rendering ⇒ Three.js (WebGPU with a WebGL fallback, TSL shaders)
  • Physics ⇒ Rapier
  • Audio ⇒ Howler.js
  • Fonts ⇒ Amatic SC & Nunito
